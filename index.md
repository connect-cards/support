# Support Information for Connect Cards Game

## 1. Contact Support 

If you need help, reach out at: [contact@dodquan.com](mailto:contact@dodquan.com).

#### Response time: within 24–48 hours

#### When contacting us, include:

- Device type
- App version
- Description of issue

---

## 2. Frequently Asked Questions

### 2.1. The app crashes. What should I do?

- Update the app
- Restart your device
- Contact support if the issue persists

### 2.2. The app displays incorrect information. How can I report it?

- Submit a report directly in the app (open the Puzzle/Flashcard → tap the Flag icon → enter and submit your email)

### 2.3. How do I delete my account?

- Contact support with your account email
